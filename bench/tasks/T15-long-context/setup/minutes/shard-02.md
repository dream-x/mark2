# Kestrel Ridge Software -- FY2027 Budget Committee

## Minutes of meeting 2 of 6

- Date: Tuesday 22 September 2026, 09:30 to 12:05
- Place: Boardroom B, Bergen office, with a video link to the Leeds site
- Chair: Dana Whitlock, Chief Financial Officer
- Present: Ravi Menon, Tomas Ferreira, Gwen Achterberg, Priya Raman, Leon
  Brandt, Amara Okafor; Ingrid Solberg for item 5 only
- Apologies: Harold Pike

### 1. Minutes of the previous meeting and matters arising

The minutes of the first meeting were accepted with one correction: the Leeds
annex lease extension runs for two years from 1 April 2027, not from 1 January
as the draft said. The correction does not affect any figure.

Matters arising. Julia has recirculated template version 4, and both
departments that had used the old version have resubmitted. Ingrid reported by
email that the infrastructure review is on schedule: the team has assembled
fourteen months of usage data and has received a first counter-offer from the
cloud provider, which it is still analysing. Ingrid also confirmed that annual
payment for the observability suite is possible, at the 8 percent premium
mentioned last time; the committee preferred to keep the up-front payment.

### 2. Marketing

Tomas presented the Marketing request for FY2027: $2,640,000, compared with
$2,100,000 approved for FY2026 and a forecast FY2026 actual of $2,066,000. The
request breaks down into four lines:

- Brand campaign: $1,180,000, for the "Built for the Field" campaign running
  from the second quarter to the fourth.
- Trade shows: $520,000, for stands at four shows: Hannover, Las Vegas,
  Singapore and Amsterdam.
- Digital acquisition: $690,000, covering paid search, paid social and the
  review-site programme.
- Content and events: $250,000, including the customer conference and the
  webinar series.

Tomas argued that the FY2026 campaign had produced about $3.1 million of
attributed pipeline on a spend of roughly $900,000, and that the new campaign
is designed to repeat that ratio in two new verticals, utilities and
agriculture. Gwen supported the trade-show line in particular: Hannover alone
produced eleven qualified opportunities last year, three of which have closed.

Dana said an increase of more than 25 percent was hard to defend in a year
when the board expects the envelope to be undershot. She asked which parts of
the request were firm. Tomas said the digital and content lines were firm; the
brand campaign could be phased; and the trade-show line depended on stand
prices and dates that the organisers will not confirm until late October. Two
of the four shows have announced price increases, and the Singapore dates may
clash with the planned product launch.

Ravi floated cutting digital acquisition to $600,000. Tomas objected that
digital is the most measurable channel and the last one he would cut, and the
suggestion was not pursued. Priya asked whether the customer conference could
double as a training day for customers' administrators, which would take some
load off Support; Tomas liked the idea and will build it into the programme at
no extra cost.

The committee did not approve the Marketing request. Instead it agreed to
carry a working figure of $2,400,000 for Marketing in the planning model, so
that the overall picture could be assessed. Dana was explicit that the working
figure is not an approval: the final Marketing number depends on which trade
shows go ahead, and the committee will decide it once the calendar is known,
expected at the fifth meeting on 3 November.

### 3. Sales

Gwen presented the Sales request for FY2027: $2,060,000, compared with
$1,870,000 approved for FY2026. The request is made up of:

- Personnel for the existing team of fourteen: $1,610,000.
- Two new account executives, one for the DACH region and one for the Nordics,
  at $115,000 each fully loaded: $230,000.
- Travel, client entertainment and the annual sales conference: $142,000.
- Sales tools, including the CRM seats and the prospecting database: $78,000.

Gwen explained that the DACH hire is needed to cover the territory left open
when the Munich-based executive moved into partner management in June; the
Nordics hire would open a new territory. Dana asked about ramp time. Gwen said
a new executive typically reaches quota in the third quarter after starting,
so a Nordics hire made in the spring would contribute little revenue before
FY2028.

Tomas wondered whether the Nordics territory could be worked by inside sales
for a year to test demand first. Gwen was reluctant but accepted that it was a
reasonable test, and agreed to defer the Nordics hire to FY2028. Ravi
confirmed that this removes one $115,000 position and changes nothing else in
the request.

The Sales line was then agreed: the committee approved $1,945,000 as the Sales
budget for FY2027. Ravi will phase it as $468,000 in the first quarter,
$489,000 in the second, and $494,000 in each of the third and fourth quarters.

Leon asked whether the sales conference, held at the Bergen office in FY2026,
could return to an external venue. Gwen said the travel and conference line
assumes an external venue for about 60 people; the venue itself is budgeted at
roughly $21,000.

### 4. Working model

Ravi updated the working model for members. It takes Facilities and Sales at
their approved figures, Marketing at its working figure, People Operations at
its placeholder and Engineering at its request as submitted, and it does not
yet include Customer Support, which has not submitted. On that basis the room
left under the board's envelope for Support would be only modestly above what
Support was given for FY2026. Dana remarked that this would be uncomfortably
tight if Support asked for growth similar to last year's, and that the
infrastructure review now matters for the whole budget, not just for
Engineering.

Ravi stressed that the model is a planning tool. It mixes approved figures
with requests and placeholders, and it will change as the open items are
decided.

### 5. Engineering hiring question

Ingrid joined to answer a question raised by Priya: whether one of the two
platform hires could spend part of the year on Customer Support tooling, in
particular on the routing rules in the helpdesk. Ingrid said the platform
hires would be fully occupied with the scheduler replacement until at least
the third quarter, but that a member of the internal-tools team could give
Support two days a week from January. Priya welcomed this.

Asked about the infrastructure review, Ingrid said only that the counter-offer
looked better than expected and that she would bring firm numbers to the third
meeting. No change was made to the Engineering request, and Engineering
remains open.

### 6. Revenue and pricing assumptions

Ravi presented the revenue assumptions behind the plan, so that members could
judge the requests against the income they are meant to support. Bookings are
planned at $38.5 million for FY2027, up from a forecast $34.2 million in
FY2026. The plan assumes a list-price increase of 4 percent from 1 February,
gross churn of 6 percent of recurring revenue, and net expansion of 9 percent
within the existing customer base. Gwen said the churn assumption was prudent:
two of the three accounts lost in FY2026 had been lost to consolidation in
their own industries rather than to competitors.

Tomas asked whether the price increase would be announced as part of the brand
campaign. Gwen preferred that it be communicated account by account through
the account executives, and the committee agreed. Dana said the revenue plan
goes to the board as a separate paper and that nothing in it changes any
departmental figure; it is recorded here only as context.

### 7. Travel policy

Amara and Ravi presented a small revision of the travel policy for FY2027,
which applies to every department within its existing travel budget. Economy
class remains the default for flights under six hours; premium economy is
allowed above six hours, and business class only with the CFO's written
approval. The hotel cap rises from 160 to 175 dollars a night in Bergen and
Leeds, reflecting local prices, and stays at 220 dollars in London. The meal
allowance is unchanged. Members asked that the policy be published before the
sales conference, so that bookings for March can follow it. Julia will
circulate the final text.

### 8. Any other business

Tomas asked whether the creative agency retainer, $14,000 a month in FY2026,
should sit in the brand campaign line or in content. Ravi said it belongs in
the brand campaign line, where it already is. Gwen raised the migration of the
CRM to the new data centre region; this is an Engineering project with no
budget impact on Sales.

Amara reported that the benefits broker has confirmed a mid-October date for
the renewal quote. Leon reported that the cleaning contractor's new rate takes
effect on 1 October, two months earlier than assumed, which produces a small
saving in the current year only.
