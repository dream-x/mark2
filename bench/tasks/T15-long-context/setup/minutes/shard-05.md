### 1. Minutes of the previous meeting and matters arising

The minutes of the fourth meeting were accepted with one amendment: in item 4,

does not change the outcome of the vote.

Matters arising. Amara confirmed that all requisitions are open. Priya

### 2. Marketing: trade-show calendar and decision

Tomas reported that all four organisers have now confirmed their stand
allocations and prices:

- Hannover: confirmed, at a stand price 4 percent above last year.
- Amsterdam: confirmed, at last year's price.
- Las Vegas: the stand price has risen by about 40 percent, and the hall
  allocated is away from Kestrel Ridge's usual audience.
- Singapore: the dates fall in the same week as the planned product launch.

Tomas proposed going ahead with Hannover and Amsterdam only, which brings the
trade-show line down from $520,000 to $275,000. He asked to keep the brand
campaign at the full $1,180,000, which with digital and content unchanged
would have given Marketing $2,395,000, almost exactly the working figure. Dana
said that the working figure had always been a ceiling for the discussion
rather than a target, and that a smaller show programme should mean a smaller
campaign launch around the shows. Gwen agreed that without Las Vegas the North
American launch could be scaled back.

After discussion Tomas agreed to trim the brand campaign to $1,060,000, mainly
by dropping the second wave of out-of-home advertising in the fourth quarter.
Digital acquisition stays at $690,000 and content and events at $250,000.

The committee then took the decision it had deferred since the second meeting.
Marketing's FY2027 budget is set at $2,275,000. This is the approved figure;
the working figure of $2,400,000 that had been carried in the planning model
since September falls away. Ravi will phase the budget as $520,000 in the
first quarter, $610,000 in the second, $545,000 in the third and $600,000 in
the fourth.

Tomas thanked the committee and noted that the final figure is still a little
more than 8 percent above FY2026.

### 3. Facilities: request to reopen

Priya raised complaints from Leeds staff about the condition of the car park,
where two minor falls had been reported in October. She asked whether $38,000
could be added to Facilities to resurface it in the spring.

Leon said the resurfacing is overdue and that he would support it, but
reminded members that he had said at the first meeting that he would not raise
it in this cycle. Ravi pointed out that resurfacing qualifies as capital
expenditure and can be put to the capital expenditure group, which still has
room in its plan. Dana ruled that the Facilities budget approved at the first
meeting would not be reopened; Leon will submit the resurfacing to the capital
group instead. The Facilities figure is unchanged.

### 4. Customer Support and People Operations follow-up

Priya confirmed that the routing work with the internal-tools team starts in
the first week of January. Amara reported that the benefits contract has been
signed on the terms of the renewal quote. Neither item changes a budget
figure: Customer Support and People Operations remain at the figures approved
at the fourth meeting.

Priya also reported that Tomas has added an administrators' training day to
the customer conference programme, as discussed at the second meeting, at no
extra cost to either department. She expects it to reduce the number of

### 6. Preparation for ratification

Ravi will prepare the ratification pack for the board: the approved figure for

### 7. April reforecast process

Ravi set out the process for the first in-year reforecast of FY2027.
Departments will receive their actuals for the first quarter by 9 April and
will have until 23 April to submit a reforecast for the rest of the year. A
reforecast is a forecast, not a budget change: approved budgets stay as they
are unless the committee formally revises one, and a department that forecasts
an overspend must say how it will be covered. Ravi will apply the same
thresholds as in the monthly reports, 5 percent or $25,000, to decide which
lines need a written explanation.

Dana added that the board wants the reforecast to be short. She asked members
to limit commentary to the lines that have moved and not to re-argue decisions
taken in this cycle. Tomas asked whether Marketing could carry unspent
trade-show money from the first half into the second; Ravi said that moves
within a department's own approved budget are allowed, provided the annual
figure does not change.

Gwen asked whether the reforecast would also cover the revenue plan. Ravi said
the revenue reforecast runs in parallel and is owned jointly by Sales and
Finance; it is reported to the board separately and does not affect any
departmental figure.

### 8. Auditors' feedback on FY2026

Ravi summarised the management letter from the FY2026 audit, received in
October. The auditors raised three points: purchase orders were raised after
the invoice in about 11 percent of cases, mostly in Marketing and Facilities;
the asset register did not record the location of 40 laptops; and two
contractor agreements in Engineering had expired before the work finished.
None of the points was rated high risk.

Members discussed how to avoid repeats. Tomas and Leon will both brief their
teams on raising purchase orders before work starts, Leon will complete the
asset check during the January re-racking, and Ingrid has already renewed the
contractor agreements for the payments integration. The committee noted the
letter.
