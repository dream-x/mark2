# Kestrel Ridge Software -- FY2027 Budget Committee

## Minutes of meeting 4 of 6

- Date: Tuesday 20 October 2026, 09:30 to 12:15
- Place: Leeds site, meeting room 2, with a video link to the Bergen office
- Chair: Dana Whitlock, Chief Financial Officer
- Present: Ravi Menon, Ingrid Solberg, Tomas Ferreira, Gwen Achterberg, Priya

### 1. Minutes of the previous meeting and matters arising

The minutes of the third meeting were accepted without amendment. Ravi has
updated the working model with the approved Engineering figure. Members toured
the Leeds annex before the meeting; Leon pointed out the re-racking that had
been planned for the new site and will now be done in place, within the
existing Facilities budget, over two weekends in January. He also showed
members the loading bay, where the racking inspection in August found two
uprights that need replacing; the work is covered by the maintenance contract
and costs Facilities nothing extra.

### 2. Customer Support: backlog analysis

Priya presented the consultant's report. The backlog has three causes: a rise
in ticket volume of 22 percent since the analytics product launched; a growing
share of complex integration tickets, which take an agent four times as long
as a how-to question; and a staffing model that leaves the late shift with
only two agents. Average first response time in September was 31 hours,
against a target of eight.

The consultant recommended three additional agents. Priya proposed two, on the
grounds that the third could be avoided if the routing work promised by
Engineering's internal-tools team delivers what it is expected to. Each
additional agent costs $67,450 a year fully loaded. Even with the chatbot
pilot dropped, as discussed below, three agents would have taken Support to
$1,182,350; Priya did not believe the committee would support that, and did
not ask for it.

The chatbot pilot was also reviewed. A two-week trial in the vendor's sandbox,
run in September on real tickets, resolved only 7 percent of how-to tickets
without an agent, against the 25 percent the vendor had claimed. Priya
proposed dropping the pilot entirely and putting the $60,000 towards the cost
of the extra agents.

Dana asked whether two agents would be enough to bring the response time back
to target. Priya said that, with the routing changes, the consultant's model
put first response at about nine hours by the end of the second quarter, close
to target, and that she would report on progress at the April reforecast.

The committee agreed both changes: the pilot is removed and two agents are
added. It then replaced the figure it had accepted for Customer Support at the
third meeting with $1,114,900, which is Support's approved budget for FY2027.

### 3. People Operations

Amara presented the People Operations request. The broker returned the
benefits renewal quote on 14 October. Health and income-protection premiums
rise by 9.2 percent; the premiums themselves are charged to each department's
personnel line, which already allowed for an increase of that size, so no
other department's figure changes. The administration fees and broker charges,
which People Operations carries, rise by $54,700 compared with the assumption
behind the placeholder of $1,150,000.

Amara's request was $1,244,700: the placeholder, plus the $54,700 benefits
increase, plus $40,000 for a new leadership programme for the twelve team
leads appointed in the past eighteen months. Gwen and Priya both spoke in
favour of the leadership programme. Dana said she supported it in principle
but did not want to start a new programme in a year when Sales had already
been asked to defer a planned hire, and proposed that it be deferred to
FY2028. After discussion Amara accepted the deferral, on the understanding
that the programme will be the first item in her FY2028 submission.

With the leadership programme deferred, the placeholder is retired: People
Operations is approved for FY2027 at $1,204,700.

Amara added that the recruiting line inside that figure assumes no agency
fees, as discussed at the first meeting. If the in-house team cannot fill the
Engineering platform roles by March, she may need to use an agency for one of
them, at roughly $24,000; she would absorb that within the approved figure
rather than ask for more.

### 4. Motion: channel-partner programme

Gwen moved that $150,000 be transferred from Marketing to Sales to fund a
channel-partner programme in the DACH region, which she argued would produce
pipeline faster than the brand campaign. Had it passed, the motion would have
reduced Marketing's working figure to $2,250,000 and added the same amount to
Sales.

Tomas opposed the motion. He said the partner programme needs marketing
support of its own, that taking money from Marketing before the trade-show
decision would pre-empt that decision, and that the brand campaign is already
being phased. Ravi added that the Sales figure was approved at the second
meeting on the basis of a specific hiring plan and should not be reopened
through a transfer. Leon supported the motion; Ingrid, Priya and Amara opposed
it.

The motion was put to a vote and defeated, two in favour (Gwen and Leon) and
five against; the chair did not vote. Sales therefore stays at the figure
approved at the second meeting, and Marketing's working figure is unchanged,
still pending the decision on the trade-show calendar.

Gwen asked that the partner programme be considered for FY2028. Dana agreed to
note it.

### 5. Envelope position

Ravi reported that, with the decisions taken today, five departments now have
approved figures and Marketing is carried at its working figure. The total
sits below the board's envelope, but the headroom is narrow, and Harold
reminded members that the security-audit question could still move Engineering
at ratification. Dana asked members not to bring any further requests for
increases before the board meets, other than the Marketing decision already
scheduled.

### 6. Monthly variance reporting

Ravi proposed a new format for monthly reporting from January. Each department
will receive a one-page report showing spend against its approved phasing for
the quarter, with a short commentary required whenever a line is off by more
than 5 percent or by more than $25,000, whichever is smaller. The report will
show committed spend, such as signed purchase orders, as well as invoices, so
that a department does not appear to be underspending simply because a
supplier invoices late.

Gwen asked that the Sales report show headcount alongside spend, since most of
the Sales variance in FY2026 came from hiring dates. Priya asked for the same
in Support. Ravi agreed to add a headcount line to every report. Ingrid asked
that the Engineering report show hosting separately from the rest of external
spend, because the new agreement bills reserved capacity monthly in advance
and would otherwise distort the first month of each quarter. Ravi agreed. The
committee approved the new format; it does not change any budget figure.

### 7. Hybrid working and the Leeds site

Leon reported on occupancy at the Leeds site since the hybrid policy was
introduced. Average daily occupancy is 46 percent, with peaks of about 70
percent on Tuesdays and Wednesdays and troughs below 20 percent on Fridays. He
suggested that one floor of the main building could be closed on Fridays to
save heating and cleaning.

Amara warned that closing a floor on Fridays could be read by staff as a
change to the hybrid policy, and asked that any change be communicated through
People Operations first. The committee agreed that Leon and Amara will bring a
joint proposal to the April reforecast rather than act now. No figure changes.

### 8. Any other business

Amara reported that the in-house recruiting team has opened requisitions for
the two additional Support agents, the replacement agents and the Engineering

7; the other three shows are still to confirm. Priya asked whether the
Hannover stand could again include a small help desk for existing customers,

Gwen reported that the DACH territory handover is complete and that the

Ingrid informed the committee that the indicative quote for the Halden Freight

Leon reported that the Leeds site's energy contract has been fixed for two
